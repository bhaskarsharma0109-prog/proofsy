### 🔍 Phase 4: Client-Side Offline PDF Verifier (Drop-and-Prove)
*   **The Vision:** Enable instant verification of offline downloaded PDFs without making a single database request.
*   **How it works:**
    *   Add a drag-and-drop zone to the `/verify` portal where users can upload any certificate `.pdf` file directly.
    *   Use a client-side JavaScript PDF parser (like `pdf-lib` or `pdfjs-dist`) to parse the PDF structure client-side, extracting the securely embedded `Subject` (metadata JSON) and `Keywords` (base64 asymmetric signature).
    *   Verify the signature immediately in the browser using the server's public key (derived dynamically or cached).
    *   **Value:** 100% database-independent, serverless verification proving absolute authenticity in under 5 milliseconds.

### 🖼️ Phase 5: Multi-Format Dynamic Exports (PNG, SVG, High-DPI Print)
*   **The Vision:** Give recipients perfect sharing flexibility for social and print media.
*   **How it works:**
    *   Offer high-resolution **PNG** downloads optimized for sharing directly on LinkedIn and X/Twitter.
    *   Expose vector **SVG** downloads for perfect high-quality graphic editing.
    *   Provide CMYK-profiled, high-DPI (300+) **Print-Ready PDFs** for students/attendees who wish to physically print their credentials on fine-grain cardstock.
    *   **Value:** Increased visual versatility and higher organic social sharing for issuing organizations.

### 📊 Phase 6: Verification Analytics & Brand Tracking
*   **The Vision:** Show organizers the business impact and reach of their issued certificates.
*   **How it works:**
    *   Record audit events whenever a certificate is verified (tracking date/time, referral sources like LinkedIn, and general geographic location without violating recipient privacy).
    *   Render a sleek, technical **Verification Analytics Dashboard** for organizations inside their workspace console.
    *   **Value:** Measurable ROI for educational institutions and event organizers, showing exactly how far their brand reaches via recipient sharing.

### 🔤 Phase 7: Institutional Custom Font Uploads
*   **The Vision:** Enable absolute, pixel-perfect brand alignment for elite universities and corporations.
*   **How it works:**
    *   Enable font upload zones in the template editor where organizers can upload custom `.ttf` or `.woff2` files.
    *   Dynamically write these uploaded custom fonts to `/storage/fonts/`, synchronizing them inside the worker's memory buffer, and making them instantly selectable in the canvas editor.
    *   **Value:** Allows universities to use their official, restricted brand typefaces on certificate layouts.
